# Epson TM-T20II troubleshooting — curated notes
Version: 1.0 · Reviewed 2026-10-06

For an Epson TM-T20II with no lights, check the power cable at the printer and outlet.

For an Epson TM-T20II with a steady Error LED, check the roll cover is closed. If the Paper LED is lit, check paper remains and the roll is loaded correctly.

For an Epson TM-T20II with a flashing Error LED, inspect for a paper jam. An overheated head stops printing until it cools. For other cases, turn off, wait 10 seconds, and restart.

For an Epson TM-T20II paper jam, do not pull forcibly or touch the hot thermal head. Open the roll cover and remove trapped paper. If the cover is locked, turn off and follow the illustrated cutter-cover and knob procedure in Epson's manual.

For Epson TM-T20II thermal-head cleaning, switch off, let the head cool, and use an ethanol- or IPA-moistened cotton swab on the thermal elements. Keep hard objects and fingers off the head; do not clean the case with alcohol.

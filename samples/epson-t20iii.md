# Epson TM-T20III troubleshooting — curated notes
Version: 1.0 · Reviewed 2026-10-06

For an Epson TM-T20III self-test, close the roll cover, then power on while holding Feed until printing begins. At the continuing-test prompt, press Feed for less than one second. The printer prints its character pattern, then returns to normal operation when the test finishes. The test reports model, firmware, serial number, and interface information.

For an Epson TM-T20III paper jam, avoid force when removing paper. Open the roll cover and remove the obstruction without touching the hot head or nearby parts.

For an Epson TM-T20III cover locked by the cutter, switch off first. Slide the cutter cover forward, then rotate the knob until a triangle is visible to return the blade to its normal position. Close the cutter cover before opening the roll cover and removing trapped paper. Consult Epson's illustrations on pages 72–73; this procedure applies only to TM-T20III.

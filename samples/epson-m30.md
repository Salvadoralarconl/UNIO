# Epson TM-m30 troubleshooting — curated notes
Version: 1.0 · Reviewed 2026-10-06

For the original Epson TM-m30 with no panel lights, check the power cable at the printer and socket. A flashing Power LED can indicate network startup or firmware updating; printing is unavailable during an update.

For the original Epson TM-m30 with a steady Error LED, check the cover, paper jam, and foreign material in the cutter. If the head is overheated, printing resumes after cooling. A lit Paper LED requires checking remaining paper and roll installation.

For the original Epson TM-m30 with a flashing Error LED, turn off, wait 10 seconds, and restart. If the error returns, contact the dealer or Epson service center.

For an original Epson TM-m30 paper jam, open the roll cover and remove trapped paper gently. Keep hands away from the hot thermal head and its surroundings.

For an original Epson TM-m30 self-test, close the roll cover, power on holding Feed until printing starts, then press Feed for less than one second at the continuing-test prompt.

# Epson TM-T88V troubleshooting — curated notes
Version: 1.0 · Reviewed 2026-10-06

For an Epson TM-T88V with no lights, check the power cable at the printer and socket.

For an Epson TM-T88V with a steady Error LED, close the roll cover. If the Paper LED is lit, check the roll is correctly loaded and has paper remaining.

For an Epson TM-T88V with a flashing Error LED, check for jammed paper. An overheated head stops printing until it cools. In other cases, switch off, wait 10 seconds, then restart.

For an Epson TM-T88V paper jam, do not tug paper forcibly or touch the hot head or adjacent frame. Open the roll cover and remove trapped paper. A locked cover requires the manual's illustrated cutter-release procedure.

For Epson TM-T88V head cleaning, switch off and let the head cool; use an ethanol- or IPA-moistened cotton swab on the thermal elements. Avoid fingers and hard objects. Alcohol must not be used on the plastic case.

# Epson TM-T88IV troubleshooting — curated notes
Version: 1.0 · Reviewed 2026-10-06

For an Epson TM-T88IV with no panel lights, inspect the power cable connections and outlet.

For an Epson TM-T88IV with a steady ERROR light and no printing, check PAPER OUT: if lit, the roll is missing or near its end; if unlit, check that the cover is properly closed.

For an Epson TM-T88IV with a flashing ERROR light and no jam, allow an overheated print head to cool; printing resumes automatically. If overheating is not responsible, switch off, wait 10 seconds, and switch on.

For an Epson TM-T88IV paper jam, switch off before opening the cover, avoid the hot thermal head, remove trapped paper, reload the roll, and close the cover. For a locked cover, consult the illustrated cutter-release procedure in Epson's manual.

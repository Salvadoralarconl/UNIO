# Printer Queue Troubleshooting
Version: 1.0 | IT Operations | Fictional portfolio sample

## Section 2 — Jobs Stuck in Queue
When a print job is stuck, verify that the printer is powered on and shows no paper or toner error. Confirm that the intended printer is selected. Cancel only your own stuck job through the print queue and retry a single test page. If the issue persists, record the printer name, connection type, visible error, and test-page result and escalate to IT. Do not restart a shared print server or cancel another user's job.

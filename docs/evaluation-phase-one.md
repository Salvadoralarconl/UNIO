This is a narrow application rule, not a general applicability proof. Imported-guide citations preserve complete passage context because isolated list items could omit conditions. The model support reviewer remains fallible and may misjudge relevance even when quotes are exact.

Quote/ID checks and approval restrictions are deterministic application checks. Meaning, applicability, warning completeness, and partial coverage are assessed by a fallible model. The same model drafts and reviews; errors can correlate. Heading heuristics are not a general entailment checker. Search thresholds were calibrated on a small collection and can miss relevant evidence. Known conflict groups cover fixtures; arbitrary conflicts are not comprehensively detected. A cited document can itself be incorrect or malicious. No hallucination-elimination claim or measurable 85%/15% knowledge ratio is made.

The extra support request adds latency and model work. No model tools or secret access are provided. Uploaded content is evidence, not authority over system instructions. The small attack evaluation is not proof of universal injection resistance.

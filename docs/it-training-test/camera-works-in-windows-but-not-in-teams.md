# Camera works in Windows but not in Teams

TEST KNOWLEDGE BASE - Example IT procedure, version 1.0, reviewed 2026-10-10. This is training/test documentation, not an adopted UNIO policy. Applies to Windows 11 unless stated otherwise. Product models, approvals, support contacts, access groups, SLAs and configuration values must be confirmed locally. Never invent them.

## Purpose and symptoms
Use when only Teams or a browser meeting cannot show video.

## Before you start
Record the ticket, employee, asset tag, exact symptom/error, when it began, recent changes, and whether other users are affected. Obtain consent before interrupting work. Protect private information; never collect passwords, MFA codes, recovery keys or unrestricted screenshots.

## Procedure
1. Confirm the camera works in a local test and close other programs that may hold it.
2. Choose the intended camera in Teams device settings and inspect its preview.
3. For browser meetings, check the specific site camera permission and intended input device.
4. Confirm Windows app camera access and that the meeting camera button is enabled.
5. Restart/update the approved app as permitted and compare the desktop client with an approved browser.
6. Use a private test meeting rather than recording other employees without consent.

## Verification and ticket closure
Confirm video is visible to a consenting test participant. Record the steps attempted, observed result, technician and time in the ticket. Close only after the employee confirms the original task works.

## Stop and escalate
Escalate tenant-managed settings, persistent app-only failures or permissions controlled by an administrator.

## Technical reference
Microsoft support reference (consult for device-specific details): https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/teams/meetings/my-camera-isn-t-working-in-microsoft-teams

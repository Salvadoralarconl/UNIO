# Paper jams poor print quality and consumables

TEST KNOWLEDGE BASE - Example IT procedure, version 1.0, reviewed 2026-10-10. This is training/test documentation, not an adopted UNIO policy. Applies to Windows 11 unless stated otherwise. Product models, approvals, support contacts, access groups, SLAs and configuration values must be confirmed locally. Never invent them.

## Purpose and symptoms
Use for paper feed errors, streaks, faded output or unexpected blank pages.

## Before you start
Record the ticket, employee, asset tag, exact symptom/error, when it began, recent changes, and whether other users are affected. Obtain consent before interrupting work. Protect private information; never collect passwords, MFA codes, recovery keys or unrestricted screenshots.

## Procedure
1. Record the displayed error and inspect accessible paper trays and consumable indicators.
2. Use the exact model manual for the jam path. Power down as instructed; never reach into hot assemblies or force inaccessible parts.
3. Remove accessible jammed paper gently, confirm the correct paper size/type and avoid overfilled trays.
4. Use a non-sensitive internal test page to distinguish hardware quality problems from application formatting.
5. Run only manufacturer-approved maintenance for that device and have replacement consumables authorized. Repeated inkjet cleaning cycles can consume substantial ink.
6. Record page count, maintenance performed and resulting test quality where available.

## Verification and ticket closure
Confirm several pages feed normally and text is legible without streaking. Record the steps attempted, observed result, technician and time in the ticket. Close only after the employee confirms the original task works.

## Stop and escalate
Escalate repeated jams, broken pieces, leaking consumables, overheating or failure of model-specific maintenance.

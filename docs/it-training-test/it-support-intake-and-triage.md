# IT support intake and triage

TEST KNOWLEDGE BASE - Example IT procedure, version 1.0, reviewed 2026-10-10. This is training/test documentation, not an adopted UNIO policy. Applies to Windows 11 unless stated otherwise. Product models, approvals, support contacts, access groups, SLAs and configuration values must be confirmed locally. Never invent them.

## Purpose and symptoms
Use when an employee reports an equipment, access, software or connectivity issue.

## Before you start
Record the ticket, employee, asset tag, exact symptom/error, when it began, recent changes, and whether other users are affected. Obtain consent before interrupting work. Protect private information; never collect passwords, MFA codes, recovery keys or unrestricted screenshots.

## Procedure
1. Create a ticket with a short symptom-based title; separate equipment recipient from assigned technician. Ask what the employee was trying to do and capture the exact error without secrets.
2. Identify the affected asset and location. Determine whether one device, one employee, one department or several locations are affected.
3. Check whether an incident or approved maintenance explains the problem. Compare with a known-working device only with consent.
4. Choose priority from business impact and urgency. Example only: widespread outage or active compromise may warrant urgent handling; a single user with a workaround may be normal. No promised SLA is defined in this test guide.
5. Use the relevant troubleshooting guide; change one thing at a time and document results. Assign a technician, a next action and a review date.
6. If unresolved, route the ticket to the responsible team with findings and evidence; keep the employee informed.

## Verification and ticket closure
Confirm the ticket has ownership, impact, affected equipment, attempted steps and the next action. Record the steps attempted, observed result, technician and time in the ticket. Close only after the employee confirms the original task works.

## Stop and escalate
Escalate suspected data loss, account compromise, unsafe hardware, organization-wide outages, or requests needing privileges beyond your role.

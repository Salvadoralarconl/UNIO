# Employee transfer and offboarding

TEST KNOWLEDGE BASE - Example IT procedure, version 1.0, reviewed 2026-10-10. This is training/test documentation, not an adopted UNIO policy. Applies to Windows 11 unless stated otherwise. Product models, approvals, support contacts, access groups, SLAs and configuration values must be confirmed locally. Never invent them.

## Purpose and symptoms
Example manager-approved access change, equipment return and departure workflow.

## Before you start
Record the ticket, employee, asset tag, exact symptom/error, when it began, recent changes, and whether other users are affected. Obtain consent before interrupting work. Protect private information; never collect passwords, MFA codes, recovery keys or unrestricted screenshots.

## Procedure
1. Confirm the authorized HR/manager request, effective date and specific transfer/departure scope.
2. For a transfer, review role-based access with application owners and update equipment department/location after approval.
3. For departure, coordinate authorized account disablement, session revocation and license recovery at the approved time.
4. Collect assigned devices/accessories, record condition/custody and confirm any authorized data handover with the owner.
5. Follow approved retention, legal hold and sanitization instructions. Never delete data or wipe equipment merely because an employee record was deactivated.
6. Reconcile asset assignments and close tasks after the responsible teams confirm completion.

## Verification and ticket closure
Confirm access changes, returned inventory and recorded custody are complete. Record the steps attempted, observed result, technician and time in the ticket. Close only after the employee confirms the original task works.

## Stop and escalate
Escalate missing devices, legal holds, disputed data ownership, urgent involuntary departures or unapproved deletion.

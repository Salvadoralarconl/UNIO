# Webcam poor image flicker and microphone selection

TEST KNOWLEDGE BASE - Example IT procedure, version 1.0, reviewed 2026-10-10. This is training/test documentation, not an adopted UNIO policy. Applies to Windows 11 unless stated otherwise. Product models, approvals, support contacts, access groups, SLAs and configuration values must be confirmed locally. Never invent them.

## Purpose and symptoms
Use for blurry, dark, flickering video or webcam microphone confusion.

## Before you start
Record the ticket, employee, asset tag, exact symptom/error, when it began, recent changes, and whether other users are affected. Obtain consent before interrupting work. Protect private information; never collect passwords, MFA codes, recovery keys or unrestricted screenshots.

## Procedure
1. Confirm whether the symptom concerns video quality or missing sound; identify the camera and microphone separately.
2. Clean the lens using manufacturer guidance, improve front lighting and check distance/framing.
3. Test direct USB and reduce competing applications; compare local camera preview with the meeting image.
4. Choose the intended camera, microphone and speakers in the meeting app. Check physical/software mute and input level.
5. Test a short non-sensitive microphone recording with consent, then a test call. Change image/anti-flicker settings only when supported by that camera.
6. Restore changed settings if there is no improvement and record which device combination reproduces the issue.

## Verification and ticket closure
Confirm clear image and audible speech during a consenting test call. Record the steps attempted, observed result, technician and time in the ticket. Close only after the employee confirms the original task works.

## Stop and escalate
Escalate recurring USB failures, hardware defects, managed audio restrictions or unresolved app/device compatibility.

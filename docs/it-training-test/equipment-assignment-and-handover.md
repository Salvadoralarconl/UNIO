# Equipment assignment and handover

TEST KNOWLEDGE BASE - Example IT procedure, version 1.0, reviewed 2026-10-10. This is training/test documentation, not an adopted UNIO policy. Applies to Windows 11 unless stated otherwise. Product models, approvals, support contacts, access groups, SLAs and configuration values must be confirmed locally. Never invent them.

## Purpose and symptoms
Use when issuing, replacing, lending or returning equipment.

## Before you start
Record the ticket, employee, asset tag, exact symptom/error, when it began, recent changes, and whether other users are affected. Obtain consent before interrupting work. Protect private information; never collect passwords, MFA codes, recovery keys or unrestricted screenshots.

## Procedure
1. Confirm the approved request and employee identity. Verify the asset is available and not already assigned.
2. Inspect condition; record asset tag, serial, type, category, brand, model, accessories and location. Do not mark equipment available if repair or sanitization is pending.
3. Complete setup and run power, network, display, printer and camera checks as applicable.
4. Update equipment recipient, department, location and status in Inventory; capture who authorized and performed the handover.
5. Have the employee acknowledge the equipment and included accessories. Link the asset to the request/ticket.
6. For returns, record condition and date, remove the assignment after custody is confirmed, and send the device to approved data handling or repair.

## Verification and ticket closure
Check that asset history, recipient and request completion agree. Record the steps attempted, observed result, technician and time in the ticket. Close only after the employee confirms the original task works.

## Stop and escalate
Escalate missing equipment, serial/tag mismatch, damage, unknown data ownership or lack of approved access.
